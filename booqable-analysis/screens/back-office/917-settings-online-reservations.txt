Learn more
Enable customer accounts
Customers can log in during checkout or when accessing your online store.
Accounts are optional
Customers can either log in or check out as guests.
Accounts are required
Customers can only check out if they have an account.
Accounts are required to browse the online store
Customers must log in before they can browse your online store.

When enabled, customers will see the option to create an account during checkout. You can still invite them for an account manually.

product price settings.
Rental period
